Common questions

Good to know

Are the doctors and hospitals real or verified?

No. All provider listings, credentials, fees, slots, addresses and contact numbers shown in this MVP are sample data. No verification has taken place.

Can I place an actual medicine order?

No. The cart and checkout are simulations. No real medicine is supplied and no payment is collected.

Is the Health Assistant a doctor?

No. It is a predefined educational demo and cannot diagnose, prescribe or replace professional medical care.

What is needed before a public launch?

Verified provider and pharmacy partners, compliant prescription workflows, secure authentication and authorization, privacy controls, reviewed data handling, and appropriate Indian regulatory and legal review.
